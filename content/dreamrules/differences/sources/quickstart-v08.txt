Dreamrules Quick Start Guide
What is Dreamrules?
Dreamrules is a full rules overhaul of the Dune Board Game published by Avalon Hill in 1979, inspired by the Advanced Rules in 2019 publication. Dreamrules was first invented to solve problems with the base game such as Guild Special Victory Condition, and frustration with the expansions released by Gale Force Nine. The goal of Dreamrules is to create interesting and unique decisions for all players.

What is a Quick Start Guide?If you’ve played Dune before this plus the faction sheets is all you’ll need to start playing Dreamrules.

What should I read before playing?
Before playing read the Faction Sheets located in the Dreamrules discord channel and “Non-Faction Rules Changes” in this document. When the game starts go through “Step-By-Step Setup” in this document.
“Combat Steps,” “Common Rules Clarifications,” and “Karama Interactions,” can be referenced if needed during the game. Those very familiar with Dune may prefer to read the “Faction Rules Changes” in this document instead of the Faction Sheets.

What should I do if I want to play Dreamrules IRL?
Many of us all over the world have done it. Just ping any of the Dreamrules council members in the discord.

Mercenaries:
Play as part of your Battle Plan. Does not take the slot of a weapon/defense/leader. Adds +1 to your combat strength and you win ties regardless of storm order. You may keep this card if you win the battle.

Supplies:
Play during battle (step 1.1) to gain the Supplies cards. These 4 cards do not count toward your hand limit and are played like normal Treachery Cards. They each represent one of the four basic combat weapon/defense cards. (Poison, Projectile, Shield, Snooper) You lose these cards at the end of Combat phase.

Break Conditioning:
Play after Battle Plans are revealed (combat step 4.2). If, after leaders would be killed/survive you would win this battle by 2X strength, you win the battle as if by Traitor. (You lose nothing, and the enemy leader is killed.) May be played after a battle simply to discard it.


Orange Catholic Bible:Play as a weapon/defense in battle. Has no effect. You may gift this card to another player with space in their hand at the start of the Bidding phase before or after the number of cards going up for bid is calculated (your choice).

Atomics on Sietches
The Atomics card can be played on the Shield Wall as normal, but also played to make any single Sietch vulnerable to storm. The card is still played in the Storm Phase after Storm movement is calculated. The force activating the atomics must be either inside the Sietch of adjacent to it. Forces in the city are not killed. For the rest of the game that Sietch is vulnerable from Storm, and Atomics is still removed from the game.

Expansion 2 & 3 Content
CHOAM, Ecaz, Leader Skills, Discovery, Homeworlds, etc are not (yet) in Dreamrules.

Tech Tokens
7 Tech Tokens start outside the game: Heighliners, Tleilaxu Tanks, Spice Production, Imperial Supplies, Customs, Spice Collection, and Water Stealing. When a faction wins a fight and doesn't receive a tech token, if there are less than 3 standard tech tokens in play, they take a random token from outside the game. The Carryall token is reserved for the Atreides fate and is unaffected by this rule. (Otherwise tokens work RAW, they trigger once per turn to give spice equal to the number of tokens possessed, the spice is collected at the end of the phase, if a faction with a token loses a fight the token goes to the winner, if all three tokens are possessed they count as a stronghold victory point for that faction).
Heighliners: Triggers when a faction ships forces (Except Guild).
Tleilaxu Tanks: Triggers when a faction revives forces (Except Bene Tleilax).
Spice Production: Triggers when a faction takes CHOAM Charity (Except Bene Gesserit).
Spice Collection:Triggers when any faction collects spice from a sand territory
Imperial Supplies: Triggers when a faction buys a Treachery Card (Except Emperor).
Customs:Triggers when a faction pays a bribe (Except to/from the token holder).
Water Stealing:Triggers when a leader is killed (Except Fremen).
(If a token is stolen, spice on it moves with it.)
Full Phase Karama
Karama cards will always stop a faction advantage for the whole game phase. Specific karama advantages are listen under "Karama Interactions" on page 18. In general, karamas are played when a faction tries to use the ability.

Worthless Fate Cards
During setup each player will receive a Fate Indicator card that corresponds to one of the 6 worthless cards (Not Orange Catholic Bible). Factions may play the matching worthless card to play their faction special fate ability specified on their faction sheet once per game. Once per Mentat Phase, a faction may trade their Fate Indicator with another faction, given neither player has played their Fate this game.

Faction Special Karamas
There are no special faction karama abilities. (Ex. Guild cannot play a karama to stop a faction from shipping, Harkonnen cannot play a karama to swap hands with another player, etc.) See: Fate Abilities.

Stronghold Shield Wall
When a Shai Hulud card is drawn add a marker to the Shield Wall. (Fremen Thumpers don’t add to the Sheild Wall)
When the Shield Wall has 4 markers it becomes a stronghold for the purposes of victory only. The wall is otherwise unchanged including shipping rates and multiple faction occupation limit. If the Storm partially covers Shield Wall this may allow 2 factions to occupy it during Mentat Phase, if this happens the forces on the larger section control it. (Ix cannot drive the HMS through the Shield Wall after it becomes a stronghold.)

Multiple Win Conditions
With Stronghold Shield Wall and Tech Tokens multiple players may reach their victory condition in one turn. If this happens Tech Token victory condition is removed, if multiple factions still win then the Stronghold Shield Wall is removed. (It is not possible for more than 2 players to win the game. The only way 2 players can with is through alliances and/or BT infiltration)

Leader Revival
When a faction has one or fewer available leaders, they may start to revive all their leaders from the tanks. One leader may be revived per turn, their cost is equal to the leader's strength.
Nexus Deck
There is a deck of 10 cards, numbered 1-7 with 3 blank cards. Every turn at the end of the storm phase the deck is drawn from then reshuffled like Fremen Storm deck. If the number is less than the current turn marker, there is a nexus. Atreides may peek at the top card of the deck.

Calling your own leader traitor.
Factions may always call traitor on their own leaders if an opponent plays them. (Ex. If Hark steals Stilgar, Fremen may call Stilgar traitor if Hark plays them against Fremen.)

Battle Prescience + Mercenaries
If Battle Prescience determines the dial, the target faction discloses the number they are dialing on their battle wheel (troops dialed + spice dialed). Then they answer yes/no on whether they are playing a Mercenaries card.

Spice Deck
x15 Spice Blow cards
x5 Spice Mine cards
x6 Shai-Hulud cards
(Sandtrout is not in Dreamrules)

Spice Mines
The 5 special Spice Blow markers are Spice Mines. When these Spice Blow cards are drawn a mine appears. Every turn the mine is there a set amount of spice can be collected from that territory. There is never more spice than the set amount there. Mines are destroyed by the Storm and Shai-Hulud. If troops are at the mine when it is destroyed, they are sent to their faction’s reserves instead of the Tleilaxu Tanks.

Combat steps
Every battle in the combat phase goes through these steps, obviously steps may be skipped if the faction it refers to is not in the combat.
1. Setup
   1.1 Supplies, BG fate, Last chance for Ix alliance ability.
   1.2 Location of battle is chosen.
2. Preparation
   2.1 Voice
   2.2 Prescience
   2.3 Hark Fate, BT fate.
Last chance for many effects such as Karama, Truthtrance, Ix fate, Emp fate, etc.
3. Commitment of Battle Plans
   3.1 Fremen Fanatical Tactics
   3.2 Infiltrated factions finalize battle plans, BT look and may advise the opponent.
   3.3 All factions finalize Battle Plans.
4. Reveal
   4.1 Traitors
   4.2 Guild Retreat
   4.3 Break Conditioning
   4.4 BT Activate Zoal
   4.5 Hark Spite
   4.6 Forces Die & Treachery Cards are Discarded
   4.7 Harkonnen Leader Capture
Setup – Step by Step
No deals may be made during setup.
If a faction is not in the game ignore their ability.
Each player selects one faction they want in the game.
Randomize player seating locations and factions.
Give each player their starting spice, place Atreides, Harkonnen, and Guild in their starting locations.
Bene Gesserit do their Prediction.
4 Traitors are dealt to every non-Bene Tleilax faction. Harkonnen may mulligan to redo this step.
If BT are in the game players cannot keep their unchosen traitor cards. BT take one Traitor Card from every faction's unchosen traitors. If Harkonnen are in the game, they also take one from the undealt traitors. BT select one as their traitor, placing it face down outside their hidden area, the remaining are their starting face dancers. BT shuffle together all the remaining Traitor cards to create their face dancer deck.
Fremen choose their starting location(s) by distributing 10 forces between False Wall West, False Wall South, and Sietch Tabr.
BG chose their starting location.
Deal each player a pink Fate Indicator card.
Ixians look at the initial Treachery Cards.
Randomly deal out initial Treachery Cards.
Initial Storm is determined by the 2 players on their side of the Storm Start icon dialing 0-20 on their battle wheels. Mark this initial Storm location
Spice Blow Deck is created. Start with all 20 Spice Blow cards. Shuffle them and place aside the first 3 Spice Blows (two for turn 1 and one for turn 2). Then shuffle 2 worms into the deck and deal a card for the second spice blow on turn 2. Then shuffle the remaining 3 worms into the deck to create the deck for the rest of the game.
Setup ends.
Common Rules Clarifications (and minor changes)

Written Notes
Only Atreides may keep written notes, and only about Treachery Cards. The Treachery deck and Spice Blow deck discard piles may be searched by any faction at any time.

Activity Under Storm
The only thing that Storm prevents is movement. (Fighting, BG Advisors flipping, etc. can all happen under storm)

Discarding Cards/Committed Leaders on Traitor Call
If you win a battle via traitor, the battle effectively did not happen, so the leaders are not committed, and you may not discard cards.

Choosing your own leader as traitor + Mulligan
In Dreams you cannot choose your own leader as traitor. If a faction only has traitor choices of their own leaders, they must reveal it to the table to call a mulligan and have all factions redraw traitors. (Note: Harkonnen mulligan is always optional except in this case.)

Bene Tleilax Binding Deals
BT may not participate in binding deals or bribes.
These are the exceptions:
-BT may give or receive spice as bribes during Mentat phase.
-BT may bribe any faction to receive binding Treachery Card information of cards up for bid. They may also receive binding info for free.
-BT may bindingly tell an Infiltrated faction they will shuffle away the face dancer card during Mentat Pause to end the effect. Zoal’s Infiltration may not be ended this way.
-Emp may subsidize BT using Shaddam’s Favor because there is technically no binding deal.


Allies
When two factions are in an alliance, they may not make any binding deals with each other. Allies can, however, pay for each other’s shipping and bidding. If the faction making the shipment/bid cannot pay on their own the shipment/bid cannot be made unless the ally says they will help cover it.
Despite being unable to make binding deals, allies (including BT) can show each other any cards they have access to (Storm, Treachery, Fate, Spice). Allies CANNOT show each other Traitor Cards or Prediction Cards.

Double BT Infiltrated Factions
If two BT Infiltrated Factions fight after Step 3.2 BT may only allow one faction to change their battle plan. (BT can give them advice.)
If two BT Infiltrated Factions are in an alliance and win with 4 strongholds, BT wins alone.

BT Infiltration + Tech Tokens and Shield Wall
Factions infiltrated by BT will win with their ally only if the alliance controls 5+ strongholds. Having 3 tech tokens and/or controlling shield wall contribute to this.

BT Half Prices + Cyborgs
If the BT revive multiple leaders in one revival phase the price is calculated on a per leader basis. (So if BT revive their 3 & 5 strength leaders the price is 2 + 3 = 5 because the costs of half rounded up) Troops on the other hand are halved at the end. Normally Cyborgs cost 3 spice, so they cost 1.5 each. (so 3 cyborgs cost 1.5 * 3 = 4.5 -> 5 spice.)

Battle Prescience + Dial Effects
If Battle Prescience determines the Dial, and the targeted faction gets hit by a Karama on Sardaukar/Fedaykin/Free Spice Dialing or BT fate, the faction may re-answer the Prescience.

Double Mercenaries
Only one Mercenaries card can be played in a battle plan. If both players play Mercenaries, ties go to the faction that would normally win ties. (Storm order)
Bidding Phase TimingThere is time for players to activate appropriate abilities/play cards before and after the number of cards in the phase are determined, before the first card is placed up for bid. (Ex. Truthtrances, Karamas, Ix Fate, etc can be played to reduce/increase the number of cards going up for bid by emptying/filling up hands or held until after the number is determined)

Guild Fate + Zoal
If Guild use their fate to play two leaders in a battle against Zoal, and the BT activate Zoal’s ability the BT choose which leader Zoal impersonates.

Traitor + Zoal
If BT call a traitor in a fight they played Zoal in, they may activate Zoal’s ability to infiltrate.

Harkonnen Leader Capture + Zoal
If Harkonnen steal Zoal they may still activate Zoal’s ability. If this happens the killed leader is returned to its original faction and BT gain control of Zoal. If Zoal is still owned by BT and Harkonnen play another faction’s leader against Zoal, and BT activates Zoal’s ability, the leader is returned to the original faction as an infiltrator.

Guild Retreat + Multiple Fights
If Guild retreat the leader they used in the battle can’t be used in other combats this phase, as well as the opponent’s leader.

Emp Fate/Ghola + Multiple Fights
If a leader is killed, sent to the Tleilaxu Tanks, and revived using Emp Fate/Ghola it is still technically committed to the original location and can’t be played in another location.

Break Conditioning and Leader Death
If you win combat with twice your opponent’s strength and play Break Conditioning you win by traitor. This means even if you leader would’ve died in combat they do not. Your opponent’s leader will die. Although the battle did happen so leaders are committed and cards can be discarded.
BG Advisors + Hajr
If the BG use Hajr to move twice, can they move advisors out of a territory, have them become fighters, then move them back in now that no advisors are in the original territory? No the territory still remembers it had advisors in it.
Weather Control + Atomics
Weather Control must be played before atomics. Storm movement is calculated, then players are asked whether they’d like to play atomics.

Bribing from Bribe Piles
When a faction bribes another faction the spice stays outside their hidden area until the Mentat Phase. Factions may also make bribes using spice that has been bribed to them this turn and is sitting outside their hidden area. (This is technically unchanged from RAW)

Double Truthtrance
If a player plays a Truthtrance while another Truthtrance is waiting to be answered the new question from the newest Truthtrance is answered first.


Truthtrance Logic
When a faction answers a Truthtrance about a future action, it must be theoretically possible for them to follow their answer, however unlikely, given all publically available information (ignoring binding deals). If a player gives an answer to a Truthtrance, and the action becomes impossible for them to fulfill afterward, they can silently ignore the Truthtrance. Factions are not allowed to take an action that makes fulfilling their Truthtrance 100% impossible. Available information includes cards that faction has in hand, cards in the discard pile, any info they have access to (Ex. Storm Cards, Spice Prescience, etc), answers to Prescience, Truthtrances, or binding deals (even though deals can be amended players must assume they will not be amended).
While factions cannot do something to make fulfilling their Truthtrance impossible, they may open to the door to it becoming impossible. For example, BG say they will play a poison weapon in a later fight; they are still allowed to play the weapon in earlier fights as long as it is possible for them to not lose the card in combat. Another example, Emperor say they will win the game after turn 5; they cannot take a game action that will deterministically win them the game on tun 4. This does not affect their ally.
It is very inadvisable to add conditions to a Truthtrance, because it gives the target more opportunity to wiggle out of it. (Ex. If I ship to this location will you…?”) The answer to the question must only be fulfilled if the condition is fulfilled, and it must be assumed the condition will be fulfilled unless it is impossible for it to be fulfilled. Example, “Hark, will you play a poison weapon if you fight me in carthag?”: Hark must assume the condition is being fulfilled until it is impossible for the asker to fulfill it, but if the asker does not ship to Carthag the answer to the question does not matter.
Truthtrance may obviously be used to make players commit to future actions, however the the longer the time between when the question is answered and when it is fulfilled it gives the target faction more time to wiggle out of fulfilling the answer. (Ex. Guild say they will play a Poison Weapon in a battle on turn 5, but if they lose the Poison weapon in a unrelated fight on turn 1 they might not have a poison weapon on turn 5.)
In the extremely rare situation where a faction must choose to either follow a Truthtrance answer or a binding deal the Truthtrance trumps it. (Although this is extremely rare because Truthtrance answers cannot be made that make fulfilling a binding deal impossible)
Besides asking questions not about the game the only other banned Truthtrance question topics are questions about future Truthtrance answers. Example, “Will you answer ‘yes’ to my next Truthtrance?”), and questions with specifics that are not specified yet. Example, “Will you ally the person I point to after answering this question?” The question must be formatted to specify which faction.

Binding Deal Etiquette
Due to the nature of binding deals it is possible for the game to become unenforceable at a tournament level. Statements about Atreides notes for example could be flawed if an Atreides player made a mistake. In a tournament setting deals like this cannot be binding, and the same obviously applies to Truthtrances. (Ex. It’s possible the Atreides thought the player bought a shield when they bought a snooper. So Atr can’t bindingly say what another player has in hand at the tournament level)
Binding deals may be amended if both players in the deal agree.
Another example of etiquette is making binding deals with a winning faction to not stop their win attempt. In general this should be avoided because if they are allowed it becomes a race to see who can stop the win the fastest. In general it is considered poor sportsmanship to make these deals. On the other hand, using faction abilities such as Guild manipulating turn order to force other players to stop a win is considered fair game and encouraged.
Also, in general games will go smoother if players approach deal making in good faith. When a deal is made both players should be aware of what is expected of them, and it helps to restate the deal in more plain language so all players understand what is meant. This game should not require 5 page legal documents for every deal, but some attempt at clarity should be made. We recognize part of what makes Dune fun is trapping people in strange deals that they must follow-through on, but also if a player genuinely did not understand the wording of a deal, especially with a language barrier, this is not fun for anyone. When a strange deal is made all parties should restate multiple times what they believe the deal means. Some bad actors might quickly rattle off the arcane text of their deal hoping the opponent doesn’t notice the loophole, this is very poor sportsmanship. When playing online or in person we also generally write down any deal we consider non-standard so no one forgets what was agreed on.
