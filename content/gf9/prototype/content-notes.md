## Battle legality

This is a Basic game in Carthag. Emperor is explicitly the aggressor and has eight ordinary forces; Guild has six. Both leaders are alive, available and not used in another territory this phase. No traitor, Voice, prescience or expansion effect changes the battle. Each faction uses one legal weapon and one legal defense.

- Emperor dials 3, uses Caid at strength 3, Crysknife and Shield.
- Guild dials 2, uses Staban Tuek at strength 5, Chaumas and Snooper.
- Crysknife is a projectile. Snooper stops poison, so Staban dies.
- Chaumas is poison. Shield stops projectiles, so Caid dies.
- Both leaders contribute zero. Totals are 3 and 2.
- Emperor wins, loses three forces and keeps five in Carthag. Guild loses all six.
- Emperor takes eight spice from the Bank for the killed leaders, including its own.
- Guild discards its two played cards. Emperor may keep or discard its two.
- Neither plan pays Advanced support spice. There is no Lasgun, so no explosion.

The battle deliberately avoids Harkonnen capture, Atreides battle questions, Fremen special-force strength and other effects that would distract from the ordinary resolution. Card and leader art should show the actual selected components. Do not substitute a generic leader or a different weapon beneath these labels.

## Artwork and teaching purpose

- Components/setup should identify real component categories and link the seven setup steps visually. A faction shield portrait must not look like a sixth leader disc.
- Shipment/Movement should separate the two actions and show that ornithopter access is assessed when movement begins. Existing board geometry is acceptable. Added printed board labels remain outside this effort.
- Battle should show both plans, leader deaths, resulting arithmetic and force losses together. Two BattlePlan blocks can show the revealed plans; the prose gives the result.
- Atreides should use its genuine logo, Paul portrait, force tokens and leader discs. Its display heading has no GF9 prefix.
- Tech Tokens should show all three actual token faces beside their triggers. Its income example needs Axlotl Tanks plus Heighliners, not arbitrary tokens.
- Discovery should show the actual Sihaya Ridge card and a face-down Hiereg token before showing Cistern revealed. Sihaya Ridge's spice is separate from the Hiereg placement in Cielago East's west sector. Revealing a location does not itself occupy it.

The Discovery excerpt does not teach every Discovery card, Great Maker or all eight token effects. Later pages must cover those rules. Cistern is a representative location for testing the sequence and illustrated explanation.
