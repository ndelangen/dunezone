# GF9 2.0 card, deck, component and terminology comparison

Research pass dated 26 October 2026. This is preparation for a differences-only teaching book. It is not new rules text or a recommendation to change the published reconstruction.

## Confirmed changes to teach

| ID | Difference | Earlier evidence | New evidence | Practical effect and teaching caution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CARD-01 | Karama now cancels only the faction's designated vulnerable advantage, or activates its own once-per-game power. | O19 p14 allowed cancelling faction advantages generally; F20 p7 lists numerous cancellable abilities for each faction; DZ1 `karama` and `karama-factions`. | N26 p24 cancellation list and faction-sheet labels, pp27-33; DZ2 `source-24`. | Teach the six cancellation targets explicitly. Atreides Battle Prescience, Bene Gesserit Voice, Emperor Sardaukar, Fremen Battle Edge, Harkonnen Capture Leaders, Guild Fold Space. Old uses such as stopping Atreides auction knowledge, Harkonnen bonus cards, Fremen worm protection or income payments are no longer listed. Each target has its own timing. This is a major restriction, not just new card wording. |
| CARD-02 | The old Karama's economic uses move to a separate Solaris card. | F20 p7, DZ1 `karama`: free normal-auction card and Guild-rate shipping paid to the bank. CHOAM & Richese already supplied updated Karama wording in the old ruleset. | N26 pp3,24-25; supplied Solaris face. | A Karama in hand no longer gives these discounts. Spend Solaris for one economic option, and keep Karama for its new narrower jobs. This is not an expansion-only change. |
| CARD-03 | Holding Solaris does not permit overbidding your funds. | F20 p7 explicitly permits bidding above holdings while holding Karama; DZ1 `karama` and `bidding`. | N26 p12 Auction Procedure explicitly prohibits unaffordable bids even with Solaris. Solaris face p25 specifies use on your turn to bid in a normal auction. | Remove the old bluff-bid habit. An opponent can no longer inflate a price beyond available funds merely by holding the card. This limit is separate from the ability to take the current card for free on your own bidding turn. |
| CARD-04 | Solaris gains a new third use, paying the battle's spice-strength cost from the Spice Bank. | No old equivalent in F20 Karama p7 or DZ1 `karama`; O19 p13 used spice to support forces. | N26 p25 Solaris card and prose, p17 spice-strength procedure. | It pays the new spice-strength contribution after battle plans reveal. Do not describe it as increasing force support under the old half-strength model. The Classic variant interaction needs an explicit decision or publisher clarification before teaching it as settled. |
| CARD-05 | Truthtrance asks about verifiable information, not commitments to future action. | F20 p8 explicitly allows binding decisions later in the current turn, while disallowing later-turn commitments. DZ1 `truthtrance` gives a binding Carthag-shipment example. | N26 p25 Truthtrance explanation and supplied card face. | The old question asking whether someone will ship six forces to Carthag this turn is no longer the teaching example. Ask what the player knows or holds. Unanswerable questions do not consume the card, but deliberately asking for an "I don't know" answer to probe every possible traitor is prohibited. This reverses a later official FAQ answer, not merely an unofficial table convention. |
| CARD-06 | A Worthless card revealed in a battle plan must be discarded, even by the winner. | O19 p11 winner may retain cards; F20 p8 gives winner retention broadly. DZ1 `worthless-cheap-hero` expressly permits retaining a Worthless card after winning. | N26 p23 Worthless paragraph, visually checked. | You may still use a Worthless card instead of a weapon or defense. You cannot choose to preserve that revealed card in your hand after victory. Bene Gesserit and CHOAM uses are separate exceptions to how the card may be spent. |
| CARD-07 | Tleilaxu Ghola can revive starred forces beyond the normal one-per-round limit. | O19 pp16,19 impose one Fedaykin/Sardaukar revival per turn; F20 p2 expressly says Ghola does not bypass that restriction. DZ1 `hajr-ghola-weather` repeats the cap. | N26 p23 card says up to five forces including starred forces; pp29-30 expressly exempt Ghola from the normal cap. | A Ghola can bring back several Fedaykin or Sardaukar. The ordinary revival limit remains one special force. Do not describe the exception as a general increase to ordinary revival capacity. |
| CARD-08 | Weather Control and Family Atomics order is now constrained by storm order. | F20 p1 says either order, with cards resolved in the order played. DZ1 `family-atomics` repeats that. | N26 p25 says either may follow the other if its player is later in storm order. Weather Control may be used before drawing the Storm card, in which case no card is drawn. | A response is not a free interrupt by any player regardless of seat. Explain the later-in-storm-order condition. The before/after-Storm-card reveal rule replaces the older dial-centric procedure, and should be taught beside the new Storm deck. |
| DECK-01 | The Spice deck changes from single-territory cards and separate Advanced discard piles to double-Blow cards and one discard pile. | O19 p5 has 21 Spice cards, p8 resolves single-territory draws, p13 Advanced resolves piles A and B. DZ1 `spice-blow-advanced`. | N26 p7 has 16 Spice cards, p11 has two named blows per normal card and one discard pile. Supplied `spice_deck.png` shows all nine double-Blow designs. | Do not perform the old Advanced procedure by drawing two new double-Blow cards. One new normal card creates the two blows. In the ordinary paired-card case, a worm attacks both territories of the prior blow, then draws to a normal card. If Little Maker was the prior resolved blow, retain the separate history question CORE-U05 rather than skipping it by assumption. |
| DECK-02 | Three new normal Spice Blow locations and four changed amounts. | O19 p4 board visibly shows the four former values, and the old 15-card roster in the checked historical assets package contains no Greater Flat, Cielago Depression or Cielago East card. | Supplied `spice_deck.png`, visually checked; N26 p11 illustrates the Great Flat and Cielago Depression pairs. | Add The Greater Flat 7, Cielago Depression 8 and Cielago East 8 as normal blow locations. Change Broken Land 8 to 6, The Great Flat 10 to 6, South Mesa 10 to 9, Habbanya Ridge Flat 10 to 9. Other old blow amounts shown on the supplied sheet are unchanged. This matters for experienced players who know the old map's resource values. |
| DECK-03 | Little Maker is a new special blow and Nexus, not Shai-Hulud. | O19 p5 base inventory has no Little Maker. The earlier first expansion has Sandtrout, with a different effect, and the later Discovery module has Great Maker, also different. | N26 p11, supplied Little Maker card. | Last in storm order places ten spice on an eligible empty blow marker outside storm, then a Nexus occurs. No worm ride and no effects keyed to Shai-Hulud. Round one ignores it as a non-normal-Blow card; drawing it while resolving a Shai-Hulud discards it without effect. It is not a renamed Sandtrout or Great Maker. |
| DECK-04 | Storm deck is now standard, nine cards, with discards and three reshuffle instructions. | O19 p5 six-card Storm deck was for Advanced Fremen; p7 ordinary movement uses two players' 1-3 dials; p16 Fremen selects and returns a Storm card. DZ1 `storm`. | N26 pp7,10. | After the first random placement, every game uses the Storm deck. Drawn cards stay discarded until a reshuffle instruction. One card uses the last wheel-users to dial 1-3 each, with a fallback if no battle occurred. Fremen no longer determines the movement by selecting its private card. The exact nine face values and their frequencies are not printed in the consulted pages, so do not invent a probability table. |
| DECK-05 | Base Treachery total increases from 33 to 35. | O19 p5. DZ1 `card-reference`. | N26 p7, with Solaris newly shown pp25. | The added economic card changes the deck, but the source inventory alone does not prove a complete copy-by-copy physical list. The supplied physical-card photo shows two Solaris cards. The scan does not justify silently assuming every other design's copy count or printing frequencies. |
| TERM-01 | Projectile becomes kinetic. | O19 weapon/defense terminology and DZ1 deck reference. | N26 p25 explicitly explains the rename; new card faces use kinetic. | Tell experienced players that their old projectile attack/defense category is called kinetic. It still covers knives as well as pistols. This alone does not change the basic matching defense mechanic. Expansion multi-label Voice behavior is a separate changed rule, owned by the faction review. |
| TERM-02 | Habbanya Sietch is now Cave of Birds. | O19 p4 board and old victory/stronghold references. | N26 p6 board visibly labels the southwest stronghold Cave of Birds; p29 Fremen victory uses the new name. | This is the same board location. Teach the name mapping once, especially in the Fremen condition. Do not imply a sixth stronghold has been added. |
| TERM-03 | Hajr appears as Harj in the new material. | F20 p2 and DZ1 `hajr-ghola-weather`. | N26 p25 heading and supplied Treachery sheet. | Treat this as a printed name mapping, not an additional card or altered movement power. Preserve the printed spelling in source citations; the intended lore spelling is not established by this research. |
| TERM-04 | Round replaces turn for the full nine-phase cycle; battle wheel separates force and spice strength. | O19 pp4-7 Turn Track, turns, single force wheel; earlier Advanced spice support p13. | N26 pp6-10 Round Track, rounds; p6 wheel diagram has a separate spice-strength dial. | Introduce the new vocabulary so "your turn" during shipment and movement does not mean an entire round. The wheel's extra dial expresses the new combat contribution, not a new fourth kind of unit. |
| VAR-01 | Classic Spice Dialing is an optional restoration of the 2019 Advanced battle system. | O19 p13. | N26 p26 says it replaces spice strength and explicitly identifies its 2019 Advanced origin. | Default 2.0 does not use half-strength unsupported forces. If a group selects Classic, pay one spice per supported force, use half-strength unsupported forces, and use the classic loss rules. Fremen's Battle Edge becomes full-strength forces without spice. This is a selectable variant, not a correction to default combat. |
| VAR-02 | Spirit World Karama is a new optional route to a faction's existing once-per-game special power. | O19 p14 requires an actual Karama for those powers. No Spirit World variant in O19. | N26 p26 and faction sheets pp29-33. | Discard the named other card to trigger the power instead of a Karama. Atreides Family Atomics, Emperor Cheap Hero, Fremen Harj, Harkonnen Solaris, Guild Weather Control. It does not grant a second use, the ordinary effect of the discarded card, or the ability to cancel another faction's advantage. Ordinary play or discard of that card does not trigger the power automatically. Bene Gesserit still has no separate once-per-game Karama power. |

## Changes that must not be overstated

- Family Atomics already destroyed the Shield Wall and exposed Arrakeen, Carthag and Imperial Basin to future storm movement. O19 p22 already says the storm's currently occupied sector is not retroactively hit. The new explanation is clearer, not a newly introduced destruction effect. Its force/adjacency requirement and the round-one restriction also predate 2.0. The storm-order timing condition in CARD-08 is the difference to teach.
- Tleilaxu Ghola was already free and could revive a leader during the battle phase for use that same phase, O19 p22. New p25 clarifies use in a different territory. The several-starred-forces exception is the meaningful delta.
- Harj/Hajr remains one additional on-planet Movement action immediately after your normal movement, on your own turn. It can move the same or another group, and ornithopter access is reassessed at the start of the additional move. F20 pp2,8 and DZ1 already state those points. Do not label this clarification as a new second shipment.
- Lasgun remains unblockable by normal defenses. The distinction between Weirding Way as a kinetic defense and a literal Shield already exists in the earlier expansion rules and DZ1. N26 p25 states it explicitly; this is not a newly invented Lasgun behavior. Shield-Snooper remains a Shield for an explosion.
- Cheap Hero still replaces a leader with strength zero, cannot be played simultaneously with a Leader disc and is discarded after battle even if the player wins. F20 p8 already requires discard. The new Actions classification makes that clearer.
- A winner could already retain ordinary weapon and defense cards for a later battle, including another battle that same round. O19 p23 and N26 p23 agree. Mandatory Worthless disposal is the exception that changes.
- Thirty traitor cards, sixteen Bene Gesserit prediction cards, six Alliance cards, five leaders and twenty forces per base faction remain the printed totals, O19 p5 versus N26 p7. Some leader identities/strengths change, handled by the faction comparison. The distinction between Basic and Advanced uses of special force faces is absorbed into the new unified default, not new token ownership.
- Spice token count changes from 156 to 144, O19 p5 versus N26 p7. This is a physical inventory change, not a stated rule limiting the bank or an exhaustion mechanic. It belongs in a conversion appendix at most.
- Printed terminology now supplies formal definitions for Control versus Occupy, Deploy, Initiative, Card Knowledge, Set Aside, Slot, Supply and other terms, N26 p36. Some express existing clarifications. Compare effects individually; do not call every new glossary entry a new mechanic. Carryalls is a new label for the familiar city-assisted three-spice collection rate. Detailed timing changes belong in the core review.
- Cosmetic vector/artwork changes, portrait updates and the revised cover do not belong in the main differences lesson unless the image helps identify a changed game component.

## Expansion boundary and questions requiring an answer

The N26 notes on p3 explicitly say it references expansion factions being developed for future releases. It does not republish all six earlier expansion faction sheets, card faces, module rules and errata. Therefore the dossier can identify referenced interactions but cannot certify that every old expansion asset is compatible unchanged.

| ID | Question or limit | Evidence and what to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q-CARD-01 | Solaris prose says Guild rates for any shipping action, but its printed face specifies a Force Shipment from reserves. | N26 p25 contains both statements on the same page. Teach the narrower printed-card case for the base lesson and retain the discrepancy as an unresolved Guild cross-shipping/back-shipping interaction. Do not silently choose a universal discount. |
| Q-CARD-02 | How should Solaris's spice-strength payment be interpreted under Classic Spice Dialing? | N26 p26 replaces spice strength with the old support system, while p25 Solaris names spice strength. No explicit conversion rule was found. Do not invent "all support is free" as an official ruling. |
| Q-CARD-03 | Do earlier expansion card names/texts change beyond what N26 references? | N26 p28 refers to Gas Tooth while the earlier expansion and replacement source call it Poison Tooth. The sample explains its Special-only type and a non-Snooper defense, but the new expansion card face was not supplied. Record the printed name as a cross-reference lead; do not assert a complete replacement-card text or updated deck composition. |
| Q-CARD-04 | Are all prior replacement-card corrections still intended? | Earlier CHOAM & Richese replaces Karama, Poison Tooth and Artillery Strike; N26 has a new Karama/Solaris split and refers to future expansion releases. Need the second-edition expansion publications to settle the full conversion. The base diff can teach the proven changes without blocking on this. |
| Q-CARD-05 | Full Storm deck distribution. | N26 has nine cards, three reshuffles, one dial instruction, but consulted pages do not enumerate all faces. Need a complete face sheet before asserting range, probabilities or exact repeat frequencies. |
| Q-CARD-06 | Are optional larger-alliance arrangements from O19 p24 retained as a supported variant? | The new book has Classic and Spirit World variants on p26, but no matching larger-alliance appendix. Omission is not proof of a ban. Keep it out of the default difference lesson and label compatibility unconfirmed if raised. |
| Q-CARD-07 | Complete terminology/name mapping for new expansion cards and miniature map labels. | The clear scan confirms Cave of Birds and Harj, but not a complete corrected atlas or replacement expansion deck. A few original map labels remain miniature. Do not infer every typo or name correction from familiarity with the novels. |

## Coverage and proposed teaching order

I read the original base component inventory, core turn/card procedures, Advanced spice/Karama sections and printed card Q&A; the November FAQ's card, revival and timing answers; the new component, deck, card, variant and glossary sections; the live old core card references and live new counterparts; and the earlier expansion inventories and replacement-card notes. I visually checked the original and new board diagrams, new Treachery pp23 and25, and the supplied new Spice deck sheet.

The strongest order for an experienced-player lesson is: new storm/spice deck operation, new combat/spice-strength model, Karama and Solaris, Truthtrance and disposal/revival exceptions, then optional variants and the short name map. Keep the full deck roster and physical inventory changes in a reference appendix. Faction-specific Karama timing and Voice changes should link to the faction sections rather than repeat the entire faction sheet.
