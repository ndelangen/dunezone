## Older source coverage

The original 24-page base book and relevant November 2020 FAQ procedures were compared for core rules, base factions, card text and printed Q&A. The three complete original expansion PDFs, 12, 12 and 16 pages, were read. Their corresponding current digital procedures and modules were checked for changed interactions; they are not treated as new-edition expansion rules.

The published core comparison uses the corresponding procedures in the 50-page Edition 2. The three expansion comparisons pin the current 31-, 39- and 65-page Edition 2 books. Retrieval supplies 246 digital page locators across the old and new publications; the read-scope statements in the reports do not claim a visual audit of all 246 rendered pages.

The [community ledger](community-sources.json) establishes nine complete BGG threads and five displayed Reddit discussions. Community coverage is a dated sample, not an assertion that every discussion on either site was reviewed.
