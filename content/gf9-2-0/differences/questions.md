# Questions before turning research into teaching text

These are not missing Dune Zone Blocks or requests for new production assets. They are source contradictions, interpretation questions or unavailable expansion procedures. They can remain visible while the confirmed differences are organized.

## Missing complete component or expansion sources

- The nine Storm card faces and their exact distribution. The book establishes nine cards, three reshuffle instructions and a dial card, but not a full probability table. See Q-CARD-05.
- Complete second-edition expansion sheets, screens, decks and module rules. The [expansion report](expansions.md) lists six announced faction changes and eleven compatibility areas. Exact Karama targets, Suboid strength, Richese sale handling and revised Tech/Nexus effects need their actual replacement text.
- Gas Tooth versus earlier Poison Tooth, plus all prior replacement-card corrections. A reference in the new base book is not a complete new expansion card face. See Q-CARD-03 and Q-CARD-04.
- Whether omitted optional larger-alliance arrangements and less visible old variants remain supported. Omission alone does not establish a new ban. See Q-CARD-06.
- A complete miniature map-label and new expansion-name atlas. The confirmed Cave of Birds and Harj mappings are already recorded; do not guess the remainder. See Q-CARD-07.

## Editorial checks

Do not teach changed rules only against the bare 2019 printing. Check the November 2020 FAQ and our pinned older books too. Safe-traitor choice, deferred bribes, legal auction re-entry and several movement/card explanations were already clarified. The older adopted Ecaz rounding and lasgun/shield judgement also need their own provenance.

Do not ask the user to settle these issues by default. First look for exact new component text or an explicit designer clarification. If the evidence still leaves a contradiction, keep it labeled in the research and decide later whether the teaching book should state a qualified reading or defer that corner case.
