# Research review

Reviewed on 26 October 2026. Independent research passes covered core procedures, six base factions, cards/decks, expansion compatibility and community discussions. Each recorded its actual reading scope and source locators. The source ledger pins the public editions and original PDF fingerprints.

Cross-review corrected these draft errors before this dossier was saved:

- Harkonnen starts with two Treachery cards in both editions. The setup change is their dealing order, not a new starting count.
- A later worm after Little Maker is not assumed to skip to the previous normal paired card. The ordinary paired-blow example is qualified; CORE-U05 retains the unresolved history question.
- The new base book already establishes Tech Token victory priorities. Missing complete module rules do not make that hierarchy unverified.
- The changed Atreides slot inspection is Battle Prescience on source page 31, not Battle Presence on page 30.
- The Atreides Spirit World cross-reference is F46.
- Fremen's new phase-wide additional-worm timing is compared explicitly with our old per-pile procedure. The bare 2019 faction wording is not falsely described as an explicit per-pile reset.
- The source's wider printed Spice Vision window is distinguished from the publisher's description of the familiar effect as unchanged.

A direct visual check of source page 27 confirms Bene Gesserit Worthless cards may act as either the narrowed Karama or Solaris. The dossier does not adopt the contrary assumption found in early community discussion. Guild shipping rates, setup shipment and the Spirit World trigger rules were also checked against the supplied source pages.

The finding index includes overlaps, clarifications, unchanged rules and open questions. Its record count is not an advertised number of mechanical changes. It gives one primary record to repeated core/deck findings and links duplicates, so later teaching design can avoid teaching the same change twice.

No application code, production records, original PDFs or transcripts changed during this research. The dossier prepares content for the next book; it is not itself a published rulebook or a certified reconstruction of every historical printing.
