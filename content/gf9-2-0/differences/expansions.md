# GF9 2.0 differences research: expansions and compatibility

Research date 26 October 2026. This is an evidence inventory for the differences book, not a conversion recipe for old expansions. The supplied 2.0 base book expressly says its references anticipate later expansions. No complete second-edition expansion rulebooks were supplied or obtained in this pass. [Jack Reda directly confirms](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3785979/article/48320615#48320615) that old expansions are not designed for complete compatibility, naming Karama, Suboids and Richese sales as mixed-edition choices. Do not merge announced expansion changes into the confirmed base-game teaching sequence.

## Sources actually read

All pages of the three original expansion PDFs were read as extracted page text, including their Q&A. The relevant old live book procedures, examples, card references and optional modules were read from fresh public snapshots. This pass did not visually compare every old card illustration, and it cannot certify every card revision.

All new expansion changes below come from A5. They are publisher announcements, not complete replacement procedures. The six summaries below describe the announced direction. Exact replacement procedures need the new expansion sources.

### X-I1 old baseline and required comparison

X1 PDF pp.8-9 says Ixians draw one starting card per faction, inspect the set, choose any one, shuffle the remainder and deal it. Harkonnen receives its extra card afterward, confirmed in X1 p.11. This is not a sequential press-your-luck choice. For normal bidding, draw one more than the auction supply, inspect all, choose one for the top or bottom of the deck, then shuffle the remainder for auction. The old Dune Zone book agrees, digital p.3 `WCPB`, block `HJLU`, and p.4 `JVRB`, block `4XYW`.

Record two changes, not one. Starting-card selection affects what information Ixians has and which card it may keep. Auction suppression affects future top-deck knowledge and its ally's redraw. Need the final 2.0 setup treatment of Harkonnen's extra card, number of draws, early-stop distribution and Richese interaction. Do not invent those from the announcement.

### X-T1 old baseline and required comparison

X1 PDF pp.6-7 permits a Face Dancer reveal after another faction wins, then replaces remaining forces. It sets no once-per-target-faction restriction. Revealed cards stay until all three are revealed, followed by a new set. One unrevealed card may be cycled during Mentat Pause. Old Dune Zone digital pp.10-12, `8W68`, `CW5G`, `P4YQ`, carries that procedure.

The old replacement is not shipment or movement and does not trigger Moritani Terror, X3 p.14 and old Dune Zone digital p.13 `VLFM`. Do not transfer that ruling automatically to the announced Revival deployment. Need Infiltration's timing within Revival, permitted destinations, storm and occupancy rules, whether it triggers entry effects, how the target-faction marker works across refreshed Face Dancers, and whether Infiltration consumes that target allowance. These are missing rules, not permission to supply house rules.

### X-C1 old baseline and required comparison

X2 PDF p.7 lists six named Worthless effects. Baliset prevents one player moving into a territory CHOAM occupies; Jubba Cloak protects one territory from storm losses; Kull Wahad prevents a player's Karama for that phase; Kulon adds one territory of movement; La La La prevents free revival; Trip to Gamont returns one opposing force during Mentat Pause. X2 p.4 special Karama gives 3 spice for each discarded Treachery Card, including Worthless. Old Dune Zone digital pp.17-18 `KVJR`, `MPV2` agrees.

Do not teach a speculative new value or add free revival to the old power without the new sheet. Also obtain the new form of CHOAM's Advanced battle-payment income and ally battle-payment benefit. X2 p.8 and old Dune Zone pp.19,21 `6Y92`, `XFGP` refer to the old troop-support system. Neither original text supplies a safe automatic conversion to 2.0 spice strength.

### X-E1 old baseline, including an existing source contradiction

X3 PDF p.8 adds half Ecaz's forces rounded up to the ally's dial, then retains half rounded down on a win. Its example on p.15 reverses those roundings. Old Dune Zone deliberately follows the rule on p.8: with five Ecaz forces, add 3, lose 3 and retain 2. Digital p.9 `NQGE`, blocks `3P2E`, `DWM2`, `PCVQ` records that choice. That old contradiction is not itself a new 2.0 change.

The new contribution decision needs full text. Do not infer from the announcement that all old Occupy rules, rounding, three-co-occupied-stronghold victory, partner selection or loss allocation are unchanged. This is especially important when the old ally's spice support becomes new spice strength.

### X-M1 old baseline and required comparison

X3 PDF pp.4-6 supplies six Terror tokens: Assassination, Atomics, Extortion, Robbery, Sabotage and Sneak Attack. Ambush is not among them. Advanced Assassinate Leaders requires a lost battle with no traitor call and a surviving opposing leader, reveals a different leader of the same faction, allows one use per target faction and is lost after a normal traitor reveal. Old Dune Zone digital pp.14-18, `WKJ4`, `5E7C`, `VWPH`, agrees.

Need final Execute restrictions, Ambush rounding, how No-Fields are counted and whether the loss applies to an entering group or all forces already present. The base 2.0 negotiated-surrender procedure already refers to Execute as an excluded effect, source p.22. That reference confirms naming, not the complete expansion rule.

### X-R1 old baseline and required comparison

X2 PDF pp.5-6 gives a ten-card cache, a cache sale replacing one normal auction, and one concealed No-Field at a time. Tokens are 0, 3 and 5; pay for one force, with no consecutive use of the same token. Black Market separately offers a card from hand and can reduce normal auction supply. Old Dune Zone digital pp.3-12, `7ABA`, `SQSD`, `LKT3`, `7NAL`, `7LNN`, agrees.

There is already an old Nexus Cunning effect using two No-Fields in one shipment, counting it as shipping one force and revealing one immediately. Old Dune Zone Ecaz & Moritani digital p.50 `CBN6`, block `S84J`, records it. Avoid calling every two-token use new: the default faction ability and a conditional Nexus benefit are different baselines.

Need the actual new token values, cache list, changes to its individual cards, purchase/hand-capacity handling for two cards, normal versus alternative auction rules, who gets each payment and Solaris/Ixian/Harkonnen interactions. No complete procedure was obtained.

## Preparation decision

The differences book can cover the confirmed six-faction 2.0 base game now. Keep an expansion appendix with the announced changes and missing evidence above, clearly separated. It is not safe to promise that the existing three first-edition expansion books can simply be used unchanged with the new default combat, Karama and Spice deck.

Before teaching a complete twelve-faction conversion, obtain the second-edition expansion sheets, cards and rulebooks. Each should get the same source fingerprint, printed-page mapping and live comparison used for the base book. This is a source gap, not a missing Dune Zone Block or production artwork request.
