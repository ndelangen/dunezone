# Page-by-page meaning review of draft revision 7

Thirty-six fresh reviewers each compared one original PDF page with every corresponding digital page. They inspected the page images, not just the extracted text. Together, the assignments cover all 36 source pages and all 61 digital pages.

Five source pages have substantive gaps. Thirty preserve the meaning through the approved adaptations, although two have minor presentation issues. Source page 7 is an approved omission. No finding establishes a need for new artwork, a new Block type or a new layout.

## Findings checked against prior decisions

The original reports contain ten findings. The parent retained five substantive corrections and two minor issues. Two remaining findings concern printed page references on source pages 11 and 15. Those references deliberately retain the source numbering under [the saved authoring policy](review.md), so they are not required corrections. The navigator and source map identify the digital sections.

The reviewer of source page 26 withdrew the physical half-step finding after checking the explicit approval for native wheels and explanatory text. Its original report and follow-up remain in the JSON evidence. The final verdict is adapted.

The review also respected the approved contents and physical-inventory omissions, Square size, continuation pages, new-page faction starts, existing headings and page numbers, native Q&A and callouts, production artwork, and diagrams supported by explanatory text. It did not reopen those choices.
