# Transcription review

All 36 native page images were inspected against the OCR. The working transcript includes 509 logged visual corrections to 3,133 OCR observations. Manual contents transcription and illustration-label supplements are stored separately. These counts describe review work, not a certification that every character is correct.

The supplied scan has missing information. Glare erases letters, a few captions run outside the photographed edge, cards overlap, and miniature or curved labels are sometimes too small to read. A clearer scan is needed to finish those passages. Do not fill them from an earlier edition or game knowledge.

The transcript marks known damaged observations with `[[SCAN UNCLEAR pXX-lYYY]]`. Its page-2 quotation also marks an omitted unreadable date line. Unmarked text can still contain OCR errors. The [low-confidence queue](ocr/low-confidence.json) holds 181 remaining machine candidates below 0.8 confidence, which may include correct text and must not be treated as proof of error. [source-gaps.json](ocr/source-gaps.json) lists the known damaged observation indices.

The exact supplied PDF remains in [source.pdf](source.pdf). [page-map.json](page-map.json) connects reading regions and stable IDs to source coordinates. The [correction log](ocr/visual-corrections.json) retains each original candidate. [Illustration supplements](ocr/illustration-supplements.json) record visible labels missed by OCR. [Contents transcription](ocr/contents-transcription.json) preserves the printed contents errors and ordering; its dot counts approximate layout.

Before authoring the final as-is edition, resolve the damaged passages, finish miniature component/legal lettering, and compare emphasis, punctuation, paragraph joins and line ordering against a clearer source. The following notes record actual coverage and limits.
