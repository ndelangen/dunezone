# GF9 stronghold cards

Checked on 12 October 2026 for the stronghold card asset, editor and built-in deck.

## Sources and scope

The CHOAM & Richese expansion contains six Stronghold Cards. They are an optional advanced-game variant. Each card represents the advantage of controlling its named stronghold. The publisher's rulebook lists the six-card component and explains the control rules on pages 4 and 9. [GF9 CHOAM & Richese rulebook](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/CHOAM-Rulebook-low-res.pdf)

GF9's server returned HTTP 403 during this check, so the PDF's appearance could not be inspected directly. The search index exposed its component list and control rules. The visual check used the product photograph on the original designers' website and a photograph of all six printed GF9 card faces. The latter is a photograph of the primary artifacts hosted by a third party, rather than a rewritten rules source. [Future Pastimes expansion page](https://futurepastimes.com/dune-choam-richese), [designer's product photograph](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/5627edd5e4b0e42d8c948a6a/1675442786481-GLSYJ4CZRTZGGO0YWLJH/Dune+Board+Game+Choam+Richese+expansion.jpg?content-type=image/jpeg), [printed card photograph](https://cdn.anyfinder.eu/assets/6mCtomrl1FQjvA9fdmqmuRMV8MoCQxD9Ph1dhhmTCDu2hsJAK9aNU6Us87Mtkd21?height=768)

## Deck rules

At each turn's end, the sole occupant of a stronghold takes its card. Bene Gesserit advisors do not prevent control. Players retain cards until the next turn ends, then hand them to the new controller or set them aside if nobody controls the stronghold. Starting forces grant no card during setup; the first claim happens in the first Mentat Pause. [GF9 rulebook, page 9](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/CHOAM-Rulebook-low-res.pdf)

The requested GF9 Stronghold Deck should contain one of each of the six cards above. Do not substitute tournament variants. The Landsraad group explicitly describes its stronghold cards as replacements with altered powers. [Landsraad stronghold changes](https://www.landsraad-vegas.com/meta/stronghold-cards)

## Follow-up visual check

Checked again on 12 October 2026 after the first release. The printed fronts have no category subtitle. Their cream gap below the title is blank, and each landscape illustration fills its rounded window. The small front emblem alone is too low-resolution to establish the towers' geometry. [Photograph of all six printed cards](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6747307/dune-choam-and-richese)

The thumbnail of designer Jack Reda's video shows the actual Stronghold card back at close range. Its emblem has two tapered towers, a short tower on the left and a much taller tower near the center, joined by a low city silhouette. Horizontal bars and light window cutouts break up the dark maroon shape. The tall tower has a nearly vertical left edge and a sloping right edge. It sits inside a lighter red disc, surrounded by a dark red and cream-gold ring. The first release's jagged three-spire silhouette does not match this artifact. [Designer video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aOYvKvs4mK0), [card-back photograph in the thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/aOYvKvs4mK0/maxresdefault.jpg)

The photograph crops the top and bottom of the card, so it does not establish the full card height or the emblem's vertical placement. Comparing the visible width gives an approximate tower height of 29% of card width. At the renderer's 900-pixel width, that is about 260 pixels. The first release's 220-pixel base image at scale 0.55 was only 121 pixels tall. A tightly fitted replacement SVG at approximately scale 1.2 follows the observed tower height. This is an estimate from the photograph, not a measured print specification. [Card-back photograph](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/aOYvKvs4mK0/maxresdefault.jpg)

The publisher's rulebook is now accessible through a retailer's mirror. Pages 4 and 9 confirm the six-card component and control rules, but show no Stronghold card-back illustration.
